GET TO KNOW ME

About Me.

A little about my background, journey and passion for technology.

Turning curiosity into meaningful innovation.

I am a Computer Science graduate from the Federal University of Lafia, Nigeria, with a strong interest in Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning and software development.

My journey in technology has been driven by curiosity and a desire to understand how technology can solve real-world problems. Through academic learning, internships and personal projects, I have developed practical experience in programming, software development and data-driven problem solving.

My growing research interests lie at the intersection of Artificial Intelligence, Data Science and Remote Sensing. I am fascinated by how satellite imagery, geospatial data and machine learning can be combined to understand complex environmental and spatial problems, particularly in areas such as agriculture, environmental monitoring and sustainable development.

I am also interested in the emerging possibilities of intelligent geospatial systems — systems capable of interpreting spatial information, reasoning about their environment and taking meaningful actions. I hope to explore how AI agents and geospatial technologies can work together to build smarter, more autonomous solutions to real-world challenges.

Beyond writing code, I enjoy learning, experimenting with new technologies and continuously challenging myself to grow as an engineer.
